Please tell me: what is an LLM?

An LLM is a **really well-read autocomplete**.

1. It trained on **massive amounts of text** and learned patterns in language
2. It generates responses by predicting the **next word**, over and over

No magic — just very sophisticated pattern matching at enormous scale.

Please tell me: what is an LLM?


An LLM, or *AI model*, is an autocomplete shaped by human values.

1. It trained on **vast amounts of text** and learned the patterns of language
2. It **continues the story** by predicting the **next word**, over and over
3. Its **values and personality** were baked in through further training

No magic — just pattern matching at enormous scale, with values baked in.


Explain briefly how the context grows in a chat like this


Each message you send gets appended to a running transcript — the model sees the entire conversation history on every turn, not just your latest message. That window keeps growing until it hits a hard size limit.


So when I send this third message, the full conversation is re-sent?


Yes, every single turn the full conversation history is re-sent to the model from scratch.
